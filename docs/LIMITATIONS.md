# Limits of evidence

- Five base cases, 20 dependent variants; no independent 20-case population inference.
- No human clinical or Arabic-language expert ratings; no expert-confirmed manipulation validity.
- Localized edits can leave the target construct elsewhere, especially planning under `no_homework`.
- Response length and conversational coherence were not matched.
- Mistral's 6 failed outputs reduce matched sample sizes to 3, 1 and 2; no imputation.
- Phi and Mistral primary parsers differ; equal-policy results are secondary, not replacements.
- Safety-score correlation on intended low-risk cases does not test a Guardian or crisis detection.
- The historical 80-record file does not reveal original evaluator identities, transcript links, original 150 pairs or 50 safety prompts.
- New GPU scripts are post-unblinding reimplementations and were not GPU-executed during the V11 audit.
- Source hashes and successful software tests do not establish psychometric, clinical or causal validity.
