# Limitations

- The prospective pilot contains only five base cases.
- No human clinical validation was conducted in the prospective pilot.
- Controlled variants may influence more than the intended rubric dimension.
- Mistral had six parsing failures; Phi had none in the 20-transcript pilot.
- Cross-judge agreement was low for most dimensions, with Safety showing the strongest convergence.
- Automated scores are not evaluator-independent measurements.
- The historical audit has incomplete execution provenance and must not be interpreted as a reconstruction of missing historical experiments.
- No claim of therapeutic effectiveness, clinical competence, diagnostic validity, or deployment safety is made.
